## Benefits

$0 annual fee card — **one meaningful benefit, that's it:**

- **5% cash back in your top eligible spend category each billing cycle**
  (up to $500 spent/cycle, then 1%). Category is auto-selected by Citi based
  on where you spend most each cycle, from a fixed list (gas stations,
  grocery stores, restaurants, transit, travel, and more).
  - Currently landing on **gas** for Harry.
  - Harry's considering switching his spend pattern so it lands on
    **transit** instead, since he thinks he spends more there — no action
    needed on Citi's end, it's automatic based on actual spend, not a
    manual category selection.

No other perks worth tracking — no lounge access, no travel credits, no
purchase protections beyond standard card-network coverage. Don't bother
re-scraping the Citi benefits page for anything else; there isn't anything.

## Transfer partners

**None.** Citi ThankYou Points DO have real transfer partners on other Citi
cards (Citi Premier, Prestige, etc. — airlines like JetBlue, Turkish
Airlines, Virgin Atlantic), but the Custom Cash is a no-annual-fee card and
Harry should confirm whether points earned on Custom Cash specifically are
transfer-eligible before assuming so — this needs manual research, not
assumed from general Citi ThankYou Points rules. Flagged for financial
advisor / travel agent to research if a transfer scenario ever comes up,
not to invent partner rates.
