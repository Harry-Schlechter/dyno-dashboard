## For travel-agent

1,584 Venture X miles is a small balance — not enough alone for most award
flights, but useful to know the conversion math when planning ahead or
combining with points earned closer to a trip. **JAL Mileage Bank is running
a 30% transfer bonus through 2026-09-30** — if any Japan/Asia trip is being
considered, that's the highest-value window to transfer in. Otherwise
I Prefer (hotels) is the best raw ratio at 2:1, and Aeroplan/Avios/Flying
Blue/KrisFlyer are all solid 1:1 options for award flights depending on
destination.
